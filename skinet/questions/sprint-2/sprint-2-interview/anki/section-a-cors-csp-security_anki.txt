Answer</p><div class="ac"><ul><li><code class="c">SameSite</code> controls whether the browser attaches a cookie to <b>cross-site</b> requests</li><li><b><code class="c">Strict</code></b> — cookie State-changing operations on GET endpoints remain vulnerable — use <code class="c">SameSite=Strict</code> or anti-forgery tokens for those. <code class="c">Lax</code> protects POST/XHR cross-site, not GET-based exploits.</p></div></div>
