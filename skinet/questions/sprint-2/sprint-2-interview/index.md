## How to study this set

1. Read each section top-to-bottom — Easy → Medium → Hard.
2. For every question: try to answer aloud BEFORE reading the 1% Answer.
3. Compare your answer against the **Red Flag** — that's what most candidates say.
4. Aim for the **Green Flag** — that's what gets the offer.
5. After each section, revisit any Hard questions you couldn't answer without help.
